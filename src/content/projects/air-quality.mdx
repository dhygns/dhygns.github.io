## Overview

A research/UX prototype that renders air quality data as ambient visualizations on a home display. Outdoor data comes from the Korea Environment Corporation; indoor air is read from the TV's API in conjunction with a sensor.

## Adaptive presentation

Rendering changes with the observer's distance: brief glyphs from far, full detail close up — a four-tier (Indoor / Outdoor / Detailed / Brief) layout system.

## Recognition

iF Design Award 2018, Discipline Professional Concept (Samsung Electronics, with Younguh Lee, Donghyeon Kim, Jiyoon Ma, Jinho Lee).
