## Overview

Live Window is a 3D AR Head-Up Display that projects driving information directly onto the windshield, integrating optics, computer vision, and deep learning. It overcomes the screen distortion and vergence-accommodation conflict (VAC) of conventional HUDs through lightfield rendering plus a custom rendering stack.

## My role

Led the development of a custom VR/AR rendering engine using native graphics APIs — Vulkan as the primary backend, with OpenGL and DirectX 11 also wired into Unity 3D as native plugins. Built high-performance prototypes, optimized shared-memory and TCP/IP integration for low system latency, and prototyped content for AR glasses (LUMUS) tailored to automotive display.

## Technical highlights

- Custom rendering pipeline with Vulkan native plugin in Unity 3D
- Real-time eye tracking via deep-learning models + filter algorithms
- Shared-memory + TCP/IP socket integration for sub-frame latency
- Targets: Laptop PC (Windows / Ubuntu), Jetson Nano / Xavier / Orin

## Outcome

Mass-production ready. Tested in real OEM vehicles in France, Korea, and the USA. iF Design Award 2024 and Stellantis Venture Awards 2024.
